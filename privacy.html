Cecil Assistant: privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Cecil Assistant is a private assistant that runs on a single household's own server. Only members of that household use it.

What it accesses

When a household member signs in with Google, Cecil Assistant may access that account's Google Calendar and Gmail, using only the permissions shown on the Google sign-in screen.

How the data is used

  • The data is used only to provide the assistant's features to the household, such as reading the schedule and adding or changing events when asked.
  • It is stored on the household's own server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • To answer a request, relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider the assistant uses. That provider processes it only to produce the answer.

Google user data

Cecil Assistant's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke Cecil Assistant's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Its stored copy of your data can be deleted on request to the household administrator.

Contact

For questions, contact the household administrator through the project's issue page.
